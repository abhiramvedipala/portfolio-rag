## Projects

### BayGuard Tampa — HackUSF 2026
A real-time multi-agent disaster intelligence platform built for Tampa Bay
during HackUSF 2026.

What it does: fuses live data from the National Weather Service, NOAA
coastal gauges, and the National Hurricane Center into a single
operational dashboard. Includes a Google Maps risk view, neighborhood-level
risk scoring across 16 Tampa neighborhoods, community report verification,
AI-generated alert language, SMS dispatch, and an address-based evacuation
zone checker.

Architecture: frontend is React 19 with Vite and a custom CSS design
system, using the Google Maps JavaScript API for the interactive
operations map. Backend runs Express with TypeScript, orchestrating three
specialized data agents — a Weather Bot reading NWS alerts and gridpoint
forecasts, a Flood Bot tracking NOAA tide and water level data across
three Tampa Bay stations, and a Storm Bot parsing National Hurricane
Center advisory XML feeds. A Final Judge agent combines their outputs into
a single citywide threat posture using weighted scoring with deterministic
guardrails.

Residents can submit ground-truth reports (flooding, downed trees, power
outages) which the system cross-references against live rainfall, tide
levels, wind guidance, utility outage data, and official Hillsborough
County evacuation zones before labeling them Confirmed, Likely, or
Unverified.

Tech used: React 19, Vite, Express, TypeScript, Google Maps API, Gemini
AI, Twilio, ArcGIS data, NWS/NOAA/NHC APIs.
